EU Blue Card vs Talent Passport

The two programmes ask for different qualifications and documents. Here are the key differences at a glance.

Requirement EU Blue CardDefined requirements Talent PassportDepends on the category
Education / experience At least three years of higher education or five years of comparable professional experience. VariesQualification and experience requirements are not identical for every category.
Employment contract An employment contract of at least 12 months. VariesDepends on the specific category.
Salary Salary meeting the applicable EU Blue Card threshold. VariesDepends on the specific category.
Qualifications Relevant educational and professional qualifications. VariesRelevant to the category you apply under.
Documents required One defined set of applicant documents. VariesThe documents depend on the specific Talent Passport category.

Talent Passport categories

The Talent Passport covers several categories, for example:

  • Qualified employeeSalarié qualifié
  • Highly qualified employee
  • Researcher
  • Company representativeMandataire social
  • Innovative business project
  • Business investor

Because each category has its own rules, the qualification and experience requirements are not identical for every Talent Passport applicant.

This is a general guide only. Requirements can change and depend on your situation, so always confirm the current rules before applying.
